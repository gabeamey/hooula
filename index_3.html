Where we stand

Ten years, and the part that still isn't finished.

RiseHI helps Hawaiʻi's keiki believe a future here is possible, see what that future could look like, and build the skills to reach it. We were founded in 2016 because opportunity should not require leaving home.

A decade later that work is three programs across the islands. Kids who had never met a general contractor, a filmmaker, or a pharmacy tech can now watch one walk them through the job — someone local, someone who sounds like them.

Our target is 80 ʻOihana courses across the 13 career pathways prioritized by the Hawaiʻi Department of Education by 2027, and double the students reached. We are 28 of the way there. That gap is what October 15 is for.

What your table funds

Three programs, one arc.

Believe it is possible, see the pathway, build the skills to walk it.

01 · Believe

Make IT Happen

An eight-module social-emotional learning curriculum that builds confidence, resilience, and goal-setting, taught through the stories of local role models students recognize.

Grades 6–12 · Advisory period · Teacher-led
02 · Explore

ʻOihana Career Explorations

Media-rich courses that take students inside real Hawaiʻi careers — pharmacy tech, general contractor, culinary chef, filmmaker — hosted by kamaʻāina doing the work.

Grades 6–12 · Self-paced · 13 CTE pathways
03 · Build

Creative AI Accelerator

Student teams learn creative AI production tools and use them to make short films honoring kūpuna, finishing with a public premiere, a certificate, and a paid stipend.

Ages 15–26 · 8 weeks · Paid, hybrid cohort
